About

Design tools that respect your time and privacy

TheFastDesigns is a growing collection of 39 free design tools — color utilities, CSS generators, image editors, typography helpers and design calculators. Our goal is simple: give designers, developers and creators the small everyday utilities they reach for constantly, without sign-ups, ads that get in the way, or uploads to unknown servers.

How we’re different

  • Everything runs in your browser. No cloud processing and no third-party APIs.
  • Fast by default. Lightweight static pages with no frameworks or tracking scripts.
  • Free forever. No paywalls, no watermarks and no usage limits.

Our tools

  • Color Tools — Convert, check, extract and generate colors and palettes.
  • CSS Generators — Visual editors that write clean, copy-ready CSS for you.
  • Image Tools — Resize, compress, convert and edit images privately in your browser.
  • Typography & Shape Generators — Type scales, placeholder text and SVG shapes for layouts.
  • Design Calculators — Ratios, units, resolution and grid maths for screen and print.
