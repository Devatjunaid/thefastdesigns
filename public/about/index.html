About

Design tools that respect your time and privacy

TheFastDesigns is a collection of 69 free design tools and 69 in-depth guides: color utilities, CSS generators, image editors, typography helpers, QR codes and design calculators. The goal is simple: give designers, developers and creators the small everyday utilities they reach for constantly, without sign-ups, intrusive ads or uploads to unknown servers.

How we’re different

  • Everything runs in your browser. No cloud processing and no third-party APIs.
  • Fast by default. Lightweight static pages with no frameworks or tracking scripts.
  • Free forever. No paywalls, no watermarks and no usage limits.
  • Explained, not just provided. Every tool links to a guide that explains the standards and numbers behind it.

Our tools

  • Color Tools: Convert, check, extract and generate colors and palettes.
  • CSS Generators: Visual editors that write clean, copy-ready CSS for you.
  • Image Tools: Resize, compress, convert and edit images privately in your browser.
  • Typography & Generators: QR codes, type scales, text tools, textures and SVG shapes.
  • Design Calculators: Ratios, units, resolution, paper sizes and grid maths for screen and print.
