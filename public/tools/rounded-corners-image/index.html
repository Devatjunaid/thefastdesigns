Rounded Corners & Border

Give screenshots and photos soft rounded corners and an optional border. Export with transparent corners or a matching background.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlineRuns in your browser

How to use the Rounded Corners & Border

  1. Upload an image.
  2. Set the corner radius, or tick “Circle / ellipse crop”.
  3. Optionally add a border and choose its color.
  4. Keep transparent corners for PNG/WebP, or pick a background color, then download.

About this rounded corners & border

Rounded corners make screenshots and photos feel softer and more “app-like”, and they help images match the rounded cards and buttons common in modern interfaces. Transparent corners require a format with an alpha channel, so use PNG or WebP. JPG doesn’t support transparency; corners are filled with your background color instead.

If the image is only used on a website, you can also round it with CSS (border-radius) without editing the file, which is easier to change later.

Tips and best practices

  • Match the radius to your UI: if your cards use 12px corners, export images with a similar radius at display size.
  • Use the circle crop on a square image for perfect round avatars.
  • For slides and documents with colored backgrounds, set the corner background to the slide color and export JPG.
  • A thin border in a light grey helps white-background screenshots stand out on white pages.
  • Radius is a percentage of the shorter side, so 50% on a square makes a circle.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my corners white instead of transparent?

JPG doesn’t support transparency. Choose PNG or WebP and keep “Transparent corners” checked.

How do I make a circular profile picture?

Upload a square image, tick “Circle / ellipse crop” and download as PNG.
