Image Format Converter

Convert one or many images to PNG, JPG or WebP in seconds. Transparent areas are filled with your chosen color when converting to JPG.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlinePrivate — runs in your browser

How to use the Image Format Converter

  1. Pick the format you want to convert to.
  2. Adjust quality and background color if converting to JPG or WebP.
  3. Drop one or more images on the upload area.
  4. Download each converted file or all at once.

About this image format converter

PNG is lossless and supports transparency — best for logos, icons and screenshots. JPG is ideal for photographs and has universal compatibility. WebP supports both transparency and lossy compression with much smaller files, making it the best choice for websites.

Your browser decodes the source file, so any format your browser can display (including GIF, BMP, SVG and usually AVIF) can be converted.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert WebP to JPG?

Choose “JPG” as the output, drop your WebP files and click Download.

Will converting PNG to JPG lose transparency?

Yes, JPG doesn’t support transparency. Transparent pixels are filled with the background color you choose.
