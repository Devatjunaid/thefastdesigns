How to use the Rotate & Flip Image

  1. Upload, drop or paste your image.
  2. Rotate left or right in 90° steps.
  3. Flip horizontally to mirror the image, or vertically to turn it upside down.
  4. Download the result in your preferred format.

About this rotate & flip image

Phones sometimes save photos sideways, and many design tasks need mirrored images — for example to make a person face into the page layout. This tool applies lossless 90° rotations and flips on a canvas at the original resolution.

Tips and best practices

  • Mirroring a photo with text in it makes the text unreadable; flip only images without lettering.
  • Faces are rarely symmetrical, so flipped portraits can look subtly unfamiliar.
  • Flip product photos so the product faces into the page content, guiding the reader’s eye.
  • Choose PNG output for a perfectly lossless result.
  • If a phone photo shows sideways in some apps but not others, it relies on an orientation tag; rotating and re-saving fixes it everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

How do I mirror an image?

Click “Flip horizontal” to create a mirror image, then download.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. 90° rotations and flips move pixels without resampling. Choose PNG for a perfectly lossless file.
