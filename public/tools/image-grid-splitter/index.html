Image Splitter: Cut a Photo Into a Grid

Use this image splitter to turn a wide photo into a swipeable panorama or a large picture into a 3×3 grid. Tiles are numbered in posting order.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlineRuns in your browser

How to use the Image Grid Splitter

  1. Upload the image you want to split.
  2. Set rows and columns, or click a preset like 1×3 panorama or 3×3 grid.
  3. Check the dashed cut lines on the preview.
  4. Download all tiles; they are numbered left to right, top to bottom.

About this image grid splitter

Splitting a panorama into equal tiles and posting them as a carousel lets followers swipe across one continuous image. The same idea in a 3×3 layout turns nine separate posts into one large picture on an Instagram profile grid.

Tiles are cut at whole-pixel boundaries; if the image width doesn’t divide evenly, the last few pixels on the right or bottom are trimmed so every tile is identical in size.

Worked example

A 3240 × 1350 image split into 1 row and 3 columns gives three 1080 × 1350 slides, exactly Instagram’s portrait post size.

Tips and best practices

  • For a seamless Instagram carousel, make the source exactly 1080×N wide (e.g. 3240×1080 for three square slides).
  • Since Instagram’s 2025 grid change, profile thumbnails are shown at 3:4, so square tiles may be cropped on the grid; check before posting.
  • For a profile-grid mosaic, post tiles in reverse order (the last tile first), because the newest post appears top-left.
  • Avoid placing faces or text right on a cut line.
  • Choose PNG for graphics with text so edges stay crisp after splitting.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a seamless Instagram carousel?

Create a wide image at 1080px height (e.g. 3240×1080), split it into 1×3 tiles and upload them in order as one carousel post.

In what order are tiles named?

Left to right, top to bottom: 01, 02, 03 and so on.
