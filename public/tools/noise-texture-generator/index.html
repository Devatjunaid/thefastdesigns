Noise Texture Generator

This noise texture generator adds subtle grain to flat colors and gradients for a tactile, printed feel. The texture tiles seamlessly.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlineRuns in your browser


How to use the Noise Texture Generator
  1. Choose monochrome, color, light or dark noise.
  2. Set opacity, grain size and density.
  3. Preview it over your background color.
  4. Download the tileable PNG and use it as a repeating background.


About this noise texture generator
Grain breaks up large flat areas and smooth gradients, which makes digital designs feel more physical and also hides color banding: the visible stripes that appear in subtle gradients on 8-bit displays. Because each pixel is random, the tile repeats without visible seams at normal viewing sizes.
A 256px PNG tile of semi-transparent noise is usually 50–150 KB, so keep opacity low and reuse one tile across the site, where it will be cached after the first load.


Worked example
A 256px monochrome tile at 12–18% opacity layered over a dark gradient hides banding without making the background look dirty.


Tips and best practices
  • Keep opacity between 5% and 20%; strong grain looks dirty rather than textured.
  • Layer noise over gradients as the first background-image so it sits on top.
  • Light specks work best on dark backgrounds and dark specks on light ones.
  • Use a 128px tile for small elements and 256px for full-page backgrounds to limit file size.
  • Use mix-blend-mode: overlay on a noise layer for richer, contrast-aware grain.

Frequently asked questions
Why add noise to a gradient?
It hides banding and adds a subtle texture that makes flat digital colors feel less sterile.
Is the texture seamless?
Yes. Random noise has no structure, so the tile repeats without visible edges.
