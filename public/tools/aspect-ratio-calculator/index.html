About this aspect ratio calculator

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between width and height. 16:9 is the standard for HD video, YouTube and most monitors; 4:3 is classic photography and iPad screens; 1:1 is used for profile pictures; and 9:16 is the vertical format for Stories, Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Keeping the ratio when resizing prevents stretched or squashed images. In CSS you can lock an element’s proportions with aspect-ratio: 16 / 9.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate aspect ratio?

Divide width and height by their greatest common divisor. For 1920×1080 the GCD is 120, giving 16:9.

What height is 16:9 at 1280 wide?

1280 ÷ 16 × 9 = 720, so the size is 1280×720.
