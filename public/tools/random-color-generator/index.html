Random Color Generator

This random color generator gives you a batch of colors in the mood you choose, from pastel to earthy. Copy any code with a click, or press the space bar for more.

How to use the Random Color Generator

  1. Choose a color mood such as pastel, vivid or earthy.
  2. Pick how many colors you want and the format to copy.
  3. Click “Generate colors” or press the space bar.
  4. Click any swatch to copy its code.

About this random color generator

Pure random RGB values tend to produce muddy, unusable colors because most of the RGB cube sits in the mid-saturation, mid-lightness zone. This generator works in HSL instead and limits saturation and lightness per mood, so “pastel” always means high lightness with moderate saturation and “dark” never drifts into grey.

Random colors are useful for breaking creative block, generating test data for charts, assigning avatar backgrounds or simply exploring hues you wouldn’t normally pick.

Worked example

The pastel mood keeps lightness between 78% and 90%, so every result works as a soft background. Pick one you like and build a full palette around it with the color palette generator.

Tips and best practices

  • Generate a pastel batch and pick one color as a background tint, then use the color palette generator to build accents around it.
  • For chart test data, use the “vivid” mood: saturated colors are easier to tell apart in small legend swatches.
  • Earthy tones are limited to warm hues (roughly orange to olive) with low saturation, which suits organic, food and outdoor brands.
  • Run any color you like through the contrast checker before using it for text.
  • Neutral greys here carry a slight hue tint, which tends to look more natural next to brand colors than pure grey.

Frequently asked questions

How does the random color generator work?

It picks a random hue from 0–360° and a saturation and lightness within the range of the mood you chose, then converts the HSL value to HEX.

Can I copy RGB instead of HEX?

Yes, switch the copy format to RGB or HSL before clicking a swatch.
