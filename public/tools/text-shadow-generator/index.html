How to use the Text Shadow Generator
  1. Type your preview text.
  2. Pick a preset or adjust offset, blur, color and opacity.
  3. Change text and background colors to match your design.
  4. Copy the text-shadow CSS.


About this text shadow generator
The CSS text-shadow property accepts a comma-separated list of shadows, which makes complex effects possible without images. Stacking several blurred shadows creates a neon glow, stacking hard offsets creates retro 3D lettering, and four 1px shadows create a simple text outline.
Use text shadows sparingly on body copy; they work best on large display headings and hero titles.

Frequently asked questions
How do I make glowing text in CSS?
Stack several text shadows with zero offset and increasing blur in the same bright color. Click the Neon preset to see it.
Does text-shadow affect performance?
Small numbers of text shadows are cheap to render. Avoid very large blur values on long paragraphs.
