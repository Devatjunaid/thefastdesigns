How to use the Social Media Image Resizer

  1. Upload, drop or paste your image.
  2. Choose the platform and format you need.
  3. Pick “fill and crop” for edge-to-edge images or a fit mode to keep the whole picture.
  4. Adjust the crop focus and download.

About this social media image resizer

Every platform crops images differently, and uploading the wrong shape means faces get cut off or awkward bars appear. Exporting at the exact recommended size also stops platforms from re-compressing your image as aggressively.

Platforms change their recommendations from time to time, and some (like YouTube banners and Facebook covers) crop differently on mobile and desktop. Keep important text and faces near the center of wide banners.

Tips and best practices

  • For YouTube channel banners, keep text and logos inside the central 1546×423px “safe area” so they show on every device.
  • Instagram’s portrait 4:5 format takes up more screen space in the feed than a square post.
  • Use the blurred background mode to post a landscape photo as a Story without cropping it.
  • Export JPG for photos and PNG for graphics with text or flat colors to avoid smudged edges.
  • Upload images at the recommended size rather than larger; platforms will recompress oversized files anyway.

Frequently asked questions

What size is an Instagram post?

1080×1080 for square and 1080×1350 for portrait (4:5). Stories and Reels are 1080×1920 (9:16).

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280×720 pixels (16:9). YouTube’s limit for thumbnail files is 2 MB.
