SVG Blob Generator

Create smooth, organic blob shapes for modern landing pages and illustrations. Every click gives you a unique shape.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlinePrivate — runs in your browser


How to use the SVG Blob Generator
  1. Set the complexity (number of points) and randomness.
  2. Choose a fill color or a two-color gradient.
  3. Click “New shape” until you love it.
  4. Download the SVG or copy the code.


About this svg blob generator
Blobs add a friendly, organic feel to otherwise rigid layouts. Place them behind hero images, use them as section dividers or animate them subtly with CSS. Because they are SVG, they stay perfectly sharp at any size and weigh only a few hundred bytes.
Shapes are drawn using smooth Catmull-Rom curves converted to cubic Béziers, so edges never look jagged.
