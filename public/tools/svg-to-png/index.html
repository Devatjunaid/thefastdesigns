About this svg to png converter

SVG is a vector format that stays sharp at any size, but many platforms — social networks, presentation tools, app stores and email clients — require raster images. Exporting at 2x or 4x produces crisp results on high-DPI (Retina) screens.

External fonts and linked images inside an SVG may not render; convert text to outlines in your design tool for perfect results.

Worked example

A logo with viewBox="0 0 120 120" exported at 4x becomes a 480 × 480 PNG, enough to display it at 240px on a 2x screen.

Tips and best practices

  • Export app icons and social images at 2x or 3x so they stay crisp on high-density screens.
  • If text renders in the wrong font, the font isn’t installed locally; convert text to outlines before exporting.
  • SVGs with width="100%" have no fixed size; the tool falls back to the viewBox dimensions.
  • Keep the transparent background for logos you’ll place on different colors.
  • For print, export at the final size in inches times 300 for each dimension.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert SVG to PNG with a transparent background?

Keep “Transparent background” checked and choose PNG or WebP as the format.

Why is my SVG blank?

Make sure the code includes a valid <svg> element. SVGs that rely on external CSS or fonts may need those styles inlined.
