About this layout grid calculator
Column grids bring order and rhythm to layouts. The 12-column grid is the most popular because 12 divides evenly into halves, thirds, quarters and sixths. Common setups are 1200px with 24px gutters for desktop, 8 columns for tablets and 4 columns for mobile.
The column width formula is: (container − 2 × margin − gutter × (columns − 1)) ÷ columns.


Worked example
A 1200px container with 12 columns, 24px gutters and 24px margins gives 74px columns, and a 4-column span of 368px.


Tips and best practices
  • Use 4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet and 12 on desktop for a grid that adapts cleanly.
  • Keep gutters consistent across breakpoints, or step them from 16px to 24px to 32px.
  • Content doesn’t need to fill every column; leaving columns empty creates useful white space.
  • Set a max-width on the container so lines of text don’t become too long on large screens.
  • Share the grid settings with developers as CSS variables so design and code match exactly.

Frequently asked questions
Why use a 12-column grid?
12 can be divided into 2, 3, 4 and 6 equal parts, giving the most layout flexibility.
What gutter size should I use?
Multiples of 8 work well: 16px on mobile, 24px or 32px on desktop.
