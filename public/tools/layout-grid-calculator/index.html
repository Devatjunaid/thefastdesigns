How to use the Layout Grid Calculator

About this layout grid calculator
Column grids bring order and rhythm to layouts. The 12-column grid is the most popular because 12 divides evenly into halves, thirds, quarters and sixths. Common setups are 1200px with 24px gutters for desktop, 8 columns for tablets and 4 columns for mobile.
The column width formula is: (container − 2 × margin − gutter × (columns − 1)) ÷ columns.

Frequently asked questions
Why use a 12-column grid?
12 can be divided into 2, 3, 4 and 6 equal parts, giving the most layout flexibility.
What gutter size should I use?
Multiples of 8 work well: 16px on mobile, 24px or 32px on desktop.
