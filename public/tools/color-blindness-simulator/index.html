How to use the Color Blindness Simulator

  1. Upload a screenshot of your website, app, chart or graphic.
  2. Choose a type of color vision deficiency.
  3. Compare the original and simulated versions side by side.
  4. Download the simulated image to share with your team.

About this color blindness simulator

Color vision deficiency affects roughly 300 million people worldwide. The most common types are deuteranopia and protanopia (red–green), which make red error states and green success states look nearly identical. Tritanopia affects blue–yellow perception and achromatopsia removes color entirely.

Use this simulator to verify that information is never communicated by color alone — add icons, labels, patterns or stronger lightness contrast where needed.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the simulation?

It uses widely used color transformation matrices that approximate each condition. It’s ideal for design checks, though individual perception varies.

What is the most common type of color blindness?

Deuteranomaly (reduced green sensitivity) is the most common, followed by protanomaly and the full dichromacies.
