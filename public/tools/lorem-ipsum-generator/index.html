About this lorem ipsum generator

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin derived from Cicero’s “de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum” and has been the standard filler text since the 1500s. It lets clients and stakeholders judge a layout’s typography and spacing without being distracted by readable content.

Tip: replace lorem ipsum with real content as early as possible — real copy often reveals layout problems that placeholder text hides.

Tips and best practices

  • Use realistic lengths: if real headlines will be 8 words, test with 8 words, not 3.
  • Test layouts with very long and very short content to catch overflow and awkward gaps.
  • Lorem ipsum hides tone-of-voice problems; switch to real copy before final design reviews.
  • For non-English sites, test with the target language, since German words and Arabic script behave very differently.
  • Never ship lorem ipsum; search your project for “lorem” before launch.
