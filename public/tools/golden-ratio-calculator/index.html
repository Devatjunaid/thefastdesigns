About this golden ratio calculator
The golden ratio, φ ≈ 1.618, appears in nature, architecture and classic art. In design it creates proportions that feel balanced: dividing a layout into 61.8% and 38.2%, sizing a logo’s elements, or scaling typography by φ.
The typography calculator uses the Golden Ratio Typography formula, which adjusts line height based on both font size and line width — wider lines need more line height to stay readable.


Worked example
A 960px layout splits into a 593px content column and a 367px sidebar. With 16px body text, the golden ratio typography formula suggests a content width of about 670px.


Tips and best practices
  • Treat the golden ratio as a guide, not a law; many great layouts use simpler ratios like 2:1 or 3:2.
  • A 1.618 type scale gives dramatic contrast, which suits landing pages more than dense apps.
  • Use the golden section to place a horizon line or key subject in photos, similar to the rule of thirds.
  • Logo designers often use circles with diameters in Fibonacci sequence (which approaches φ) to build curves.
  • Round results to your spacing grid so designs stay practical to build.

Frequently asked questions
What is the golden ratio number?
φ (phi) = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.6180339887.
How do I use the golden ratio in web design?
Split layouts 61.8/38.2, scale headings by 1.618, and use it to size spacing and images for natural harmony.
