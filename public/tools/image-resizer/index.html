Image Resizer

Resize images for websites, social media or email without installing software. Your photos are processed locally and never uploaded.

How to use the Image Resizer

  1. Upload, drag & drop or paste an image.
  2. Enter a new width or height — the other side updates automatically when the ratio is locked.
  3. Or click a percentage preset like 50%.
  4. Choose the output format and quality, then click “Resize & download”.

About this image resizer

Oversized images are the number-one cause of slow websites. Resizing a 4000px camera photo down to the size it is actually displayed (often 1200–1600px) can cut its file size by 80% or more. This resizer uses the browser’s high-quality image smoothing for crisp results.

Common sizes: 1920×1080 for full-HD hero images, 1200×630 for Open Graph link previews, 1080×1080 for Instagram posts and 800px wide for email newsletters.

Tips and best practices

  • Resize to the largest size the image is displayed at, times two for high-density screens.
  • For responsive sites, export a few widths (for example 640, 1280 and 1920px) and serve them with srcset.
  • Downscaling by more than half in one step can lose detail; very large reductions look sharper if done in two passes.
  • Keep the original file: resizing down is destructive, and you can’t get detail back later.
  • PNG is lossless but heavy for photos; resize photos to WebP or JPG instead.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Enlarging beyond the original size adds no new detail and may look soft.

Is there a file size limit?

No upload limit exists because nothing is uploaded. Very large images depend only on your device’s memory.

Are my images stored?

No. All processing happens in your browser; files never leave your computer.

Private by design. This tool processes everything on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
