About this ppi / dpi screen calculator

Pixel density, measured in pixels per inch (PPI), determines how sharp a display looks. It is calculated by dividing the diagonal resolution in pixels (√(w² + h²)) by the diagonal size in inches. Phones typically exceed 400 PPI, laptops range from 130–250 PPI and desktop monitors from 90–220 PPI.

Designers use PPI to understand how large UI elements will physically appear and to decide which @2x or @3x image assets to export.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between PPI and DPI?

PPI (pixels per inch) describes screens; DPI (dots per inch) describes printers. They are often used interchangeably in everyday speech.

What PPI is considered retina?

It depends on viewing distance. Around 300 PPI for phones held at 25–30cm and around 110–220 PPI for monitors viewed from 50–70cm.
