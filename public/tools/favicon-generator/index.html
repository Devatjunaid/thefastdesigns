About this favicon generator
A favicon appears in browser tabs, bookmarks, search results and on phone home screens. Modern websites need a few sizes: favicon.ico (containing 16, 32 and 48px) for legacy support, 32×32 and 16×16 PNGs, a 180×180 Apple touch icon for iOS and 192/512px icons for Android and PWAs.
Simple, bold shapes and one or two letters read best at tiny sizes — check the 16px preview before you download.


Tips and best practices
  • Design for 16×16 first; fine details and thin strokes disappear at that size.
  • A single bold letter or simple symbol is more recognisable in a crowded tab bar than a full logo.
  • Use the SVG favicon for modern browsers and keep favicon.ico for older ones and some crawlers.
  • Give the Apple touch icon a solid background; iOS fills transparency with black.
  • Browsers cache favicons aggressively; add a version query (favicon.ico?v=2) when you update it.

What size should a favicon be?
Provide 32×32 and 16×16 for browsers, 180×180 for Apple devices and 192×192 / 512×512 for Android. This tool generates all of them.
Does favicon.ico support multiple sizes?
Yes. The generated .ico file contains 16, 32 and 48 pixel versions.
