How to use the Image Cropper

  1. Upload, drop or paste the image you want to crop.
  2. Choose an aspect ratio or keep it free.
  3. Drag the box to reposition it and pull the corner handles to resize.
  4. Click “Crop & download” to save the full-resolution result.

About this image cropper

Cropping improves composition and helps images fit the exact frames required by social platforms. Popular ratios include 1:1 for profile pictures and grid posts, 4:5 for portrait Instagram posts, 9:16 for Stories, Reels and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails and 1.91:1 for link previews.

The crop is applied to the original full-resolution image, not the on-screen preview, so you never lose detail.

Worked example

Choose 4:5 for an Instagram portrait post. A 3000 × 4000 photo cropped to 4:5 at full width becomes 3000 × 3750, which you can then resize to 1080 × 1350.

Tips and best practices

  • Use the rule of thirds: place the subject on one of the grid lines rather than dead center.
  • Leave breathing room in the direction a person is looking or moving.
  • Crop out distracting edges and objects before applying any other edit.
  • Crop the original, then resize; cropping a small version throws away resolution.
  • For profile pictures that will be shown as circles, keep the face centered with extra space around it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I crop an image to a square?

Select the 1:1 aspect ratio, position the box and click Crop & download.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The cropped area is exported at the original pixel resolution.
