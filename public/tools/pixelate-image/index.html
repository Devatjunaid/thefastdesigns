About this pixelate & blur image

Before posting a screenshot, check it for email addresses, phone numbers, order IDs, faces, license plates and browser tabs. Pixelation and blur hide these details, and the new pixels are written into the exported image, so the original information is not recoverable from the file.

Be aware that light blur and large pixel blocks over short text (like a single number) can sometimes be reversed by guessing. For truly sensitive information such as passwords or card numbers, use the solid black box, which removes the data completely.

Tips and best practices

  • Use the solid box for passwords, card numbers and ID numbers; it’s the only effect that can’t be guessed back.
  • Pixelate with a high strength for faces; small blocks can leave a face recognisable.
  • Check every corner of a screenshot: notification badges and browser tabs often reveal names.
  • Download as PNG for screenshots to keep the rest of the text crisp.
  • Whole-image pixelation with a medium strength creates a quick 8-bit pixel art look.
