Color Scale Generator

Design systems need more than one shade per color. Enter your brand color to get eleven balanced steps from 50 to 950, ready for CSS variables or Tailwind.

How to use the Color Scale Generator
  1. Enter your brand color.
  2. Give the scale a name such as “brand” or “primary”.
  3. Keep the option checked to place your exact color at the nearest step.
  4. Copy the CSS variables or the Tailwind config snippet.


About this color scale generator
Modern design systems such as Tailwind CSS, Material and Radix describe each color as a numbered scale. Low numbers are light tints for backgrounds, the middle (around 500) is the base color, and high numbers are dark shades for text and hover states. This generator keeps the hue constant and steps through fixed lightness targets, easing saturation slightly at the extremes so the lightest tints don’t look neon.


Tips and best practices
  • Use 50–100 for page and card backgrounds, 200–300 for borders, 500–600 for buttons and 700–900 for text on light surfaces.
  • Check text contrast for each pairing you use; 600 on white often passes AA, but it depends on the hue.
  • Yellows and cyans are light by nature; you may want to shift their 500 step darker for usable buttons.
  • Generate a matching neutral scale by entering a grey with a slight tint of your brand hue.
  • Keep step names consistent across all colors so components can swap palettes easily.

Frequently asked questions
What does 500 mean in a color scale?
It is the middle, “base” step. Lower numbers are lighter and higher numbers are darker.
Can I use this with Tailwind CSS?
Yes. Copy the Tailwind snippet into theme.extend.colors in your tailwind.config.js, or use the CSS variables with Tailwind v4.
