Add Watermark to Image

Brand and protect your photography and design previews. Add your name or website as a watermark, position it anywhere or repeat it across the image.

100% freeNo sign-upWorks offlinePrivate — runs in your browser

How to use the Add Watermark to Image

  1. Upload, drop or paste the photo you want to protect.
  2. Type your watermark text — your name, brand or website.
  3. Choose position, size, opacity, color and rotation, or tile it.
  4. Download the watermarked image at full resolution.

About this add watermark to image

Watermarking discourages unauthorised use of your photos and design previews and keeps your brand attached when images are shared. A tiled, semi-transparent watermark is the hardest to crop out, while a small corner watermark is better for portfolio pieces where the image itself should shine.

What opacity should a watermark have?

Between 30% and 60% is usually visible without distracting from the image.
