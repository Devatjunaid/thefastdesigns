About this duotone image generator

Duotone is a printing technique that uses two inks. Digitally, it works as a “gradient map”: each pixel’s brightness is calculated and then mapped onto a gradient between two colors. Dark areas take the shadow color and bright areas take the highlight color.

Brands use duotone to make photos from different sources look like one consistent set, and to put text over images without fighting busy colors.

Worked example

Navy #023047 for shadows and gold #ffb703 for highlights turns a portrait into a high-contrast poster image that white headline text can sit on.

Tips and best practices

  • Use a dark shadow color and a light highlight color; two mid-tones produce a flat, low-contrast image.
  • Photos with strong lighting and simple backgrounds give the most graphic result.
  • Apply the same two colors to every image in a campaign for instant visual consistency.
  • Raise contrast slightly (110–130%) to avoid muddy mid-tones.
  • Duotone images with a dark shadow color make excellent backgrounds for white headline text.

Frequently asked questions

What is a duotone image?

An image made of two colors, where shadows use one color and highlights use the other, based on each pixel’s brightness.

Can I use my brand colors?

Yes. Enter any two HEX codes in the shadow and highlight fields.
