Privacy

Privacy policy

Your privacy is built into how TheFastDesigns works.

Your files stay on your device

All tools process images, colors and text locally in your web browser. Files you select are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

No accounts and no personal data

We do not require registration and we do not collect names, email addresses or other personal information through our tools.

No third-party scripts

Our pages do not load external fonts, analytics trackers or advertising scripts. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) may be processed by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

Local storage

Tools do not store your work. Downloads you create are saved directly by your browser.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us through the website.
