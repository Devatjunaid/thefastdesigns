Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information TheFastDesigns (thefastdesigns.online) handles when you use the website. The short version: our tools run on your device, and we don’t collect personal data through them.

1. Files and data you use in the tools

All tools process images, colors, text and other input locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Files you select, drop or paste are never uploaded to our servers or sent to any third party. When you close or reload the page, that data is gone. Files you download are created by your browser on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details or similar personal information through the tools. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social media pixels.

3. Server logs

Like every website, our pages are delivered by a hosting provider (Cloudflare). To deliver pages securely and protect against abuse, the provider may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of the request. This data is handled under Cloudflare’s own privacy policy, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website does not set cookies for tracking. The tools don’t store your work in your browser. Your hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies.

5. Emails you send us

If you email us at elonopo@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the website, we don’t add you to any mailing list, and we delete correspondence when it’s no longer needed. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

6. External links

Some pages link to other websites (for example a map link in the EXIF viewer, which you choose to open). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we don’t control.

7. Children

The website is a general design resource and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we don’t collect personal data through the tools, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can request a copy or deletion of that correspondence by writing to elonopo@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: elonopo@gmail.com.
